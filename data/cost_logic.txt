DUBAI RENTAL COST CALCULATOR — COMPLETE MOVE-IN COST GUIDE
Reference: Dubai real estate market standard costs (2024)

========================================
SECTION 1 — MOVE-IN COST FORMULA
========================================

Total move-in cost = Annual Rent + Security Deposit + Agency Fee + Ejari + DEWA Deposit + Chiller Deposit (if applicable)

FORMULA:
  Security Deposit     = Annual Rent × 5%   (unfurnished)
  Security Deposit     = Annual Rent × 10%  (furnished)
  Agency Fee           = Annual Rent × 5%   (standard — negotiable)
  Ejari Registration   = AED 220 (fixed government fee)
  DEWA Deposit         = AED 2,000 (apartment) or AED 4,000 (villa)
  Chiller Deposit      = AED 1,000 to AED 2,000 (only in district cooling areas)
  Moving Cost estimate = AED 1,500 to AED 5,000 (optional, depends on volume)

========================================
SECTION 3 — ONGOING MONTHLY COSTS
========================================

After move-in, expect these monthly costs on top of rent:

DEWA (electricity + water):
  Studio:       AED 250 to 400/month
  1 Bedroom:    AED 350 to 600/month
  2 Bedroom:    AED 500 to 900/month
  3 Bedroom:    AED 700 to 1,200/month
  Villa:        AED 1,000 to 3,000/month (depending on pool, garden)

District Cooling (Chiller) — applies in areas like Business Bay, DIFC, Downtown, JBR, Palm:
  Studio:       AED 300 to 500/month
  1 Bedroom:    AED 450 to 700/month
  2 Bedroom:    AED 600 to 1,000/month
  Note: Chiller is billed separately from DEWA in district-cooled buildings.

Internet:
  Standard package: AED 300 to 500/month (Etisalat or du)

Service Charges (paid by owner, sometimes passed to tenant in rent):
  Average: AED 10 to 25 per square foot per year
  Example: 800 sqft apartment × AED 15 = AED 12,000/year = AED 1,000/month added to rent

Municipality Tax (Housing Fee):
  5% of annual rent, collected by DEWA monthly
  Example: AED 70,000 rent = AED 3,500/year = AED 292/month added to DEWA bill

========================================
SECTION 4 — COST SAVING TIPS
========================================

1. Pay in fewer cheques: Offering 1 or 2 cheques instead of 4 gives negotiating power. Can save 5 to 10% on rent.

2. Negotiate agency fee: In a soft market or vacant unit, agency fee is negotiable. Some landlords cover it.

3. Avoid furnished if you have furniture: Furnished adds 5% to deposit and typically 20 to 40% to rent.

4. Check if chiller is included: Some buildings include chiller in service charges. Ask before signing.

5. Compare Ejari renewal vs new contract: Renewing existing contract is cheaper than a new registration.

6. Time your move: Q1 (Jan-March) tends to have more vacancies and negotiable prices. Q3 (July-Sept) is high season.
